======================================
          WINGS OF FURY  STE
@BUILD@
======================================

A conversion of the Amiga game for the
Atari STE and Mega STE. It follows the
original as closely as the machine
allows.

--------------------------------------
 WHAT YOU NEED
--------------------------------------

 * Atari STE or Mega STE
 * 2 MB of memory or more
 * Colour monitor or TV
 * Joystick in port 1 (the second
   port), or the keyboard

--------------------------------------
 STARTING
--------------------------------------

Floppy:
  Boot from the disk. The game starts
  by itself.

Hard disk:
  Copy the folder to the hard disk
  and start WOF.PRG.

  WOF.PRG   the game
  DATA      the game's files
  SAVE      saved games, high scores

A disk symbol at the top right shows
that the game is loading.

--------------------------------------
 THE GAME
--------------------------------------

You fly a Hellcat from an aircraft
carrier in the Pacific. Choose your
rank, read the mission briefing,
pick a weapon on the deck and take
off.

A mission is complete when all enemy
islands are cleared and all enemy
ships are sunk. Then return to the
carrier and land.

  Islands  Destroy the guns, the
           bunkers and the troops.
  Ships    Sink them with torpedoes,
           bombs or rockets.
  Planes   Shoot down enemy fighters
           and keep bombers away
           from your carrier.

Watch the fuel and oil gauges. Land
on the carrier to refuel, repair and
re-arm. The mission is lost when you
run out of planes or the carrier is
sunk.

--------------------------------------
 CONTROLS
--------------------------------------

In the air:
  Up / down      pitch
  Towards nose   more throttle
  Towards tail   turn around
  Fire, tap      bomb, rocket or
                 torpedo
  Fire, hold     machine gun

On the deck:
  Up / down      choose the weapon
  Fire           go

Keyboard instead of the joystick:
  cursor keys and space.

--------------------------------------
 KEYS
--------------------------------------

  Esc        pause
  H          help page
  Ctrl+R     back to rank selection
  Ctrl+G     save game (on the deck)
  Ctrl+L     load game
  Ctrl+S     sound off / on
  Ctrl+F     swap stick up / down
  Ctrl+C     clear the high scores
  Ctrl+V     show the version
  Ctrl+Q     quit

Fire or any key skips the intro text
and the title pictures.

--------------------------------------
 SAVED GAMES
--------------------------------------

You can save while the plane stands
on the carrier deck. Press Ctrl+G,
pick one of six slots, type a name
and press Return.

To load, press Ctrl+L, or choose
RETURN FROM R&R on the rank screen.

A saved game holds the mission as it
stands: what is destroyed stays
destroyed.

The disk must not be write protected.
If saving fails the game tells you
and carries on.

--------------------------------------
 CHEAT KEYS
--------------------------------------

As in the Amiga version. During a
mission type

  c o l i n

(typing "colin was here!" works too,
only the first word counts). Then:

  M        unlimited weapons on / off
  C        next weapon
  R        remove all bombs, rockets
           and shots in flight
  F        fuel to two thirds
  D        full oil, and no damage
           from guns and planes
           on / off
  P        one more plane
  8 / 2    bombs fall faster / slower
  6 / 4    the same in small steps
  I / K    plane pitches faster /
           slower
  Q        quit
  F10      clear the message line
  Help     soldiers and pillboxes on
           the island below

The keys stay active until you quit.

--------------------------------------
 DIFFERENCES TO THE AMIGA VERSION
--------------------------------------

 * 16 colours on screen instead of
   32. The pictures were reduced to
   16 colours.
 * The mission briefing and the hall
   of fame are shown in low
   resolution.
 * Names and the save dialog use a
   small font in capital letters.
 * Saved games are files SAVE1.WOF
   to SAVE6.WOF. Names can be up to
   16 characters long.
 * A loading screen with a disk
   symbol appears before each
   mission and menu picture.
 * Esc shows a pause page. The Amiga
   version pauses without showing
   anything.
 * H shows a help page and Ctrl+Q
   quits. The Amiga version has
   neither.
 * Not included: the demo mode and
   the cheat key Space (memory
   figures: Space is fire here).
 * Sound and music use the original
   samples, played at 12.5 kHz.

--------------------------------------
 KNOWN ISSUES
--------------------------------------

 * On some machines the sound has
   faint clicks or crackle while it
   plays. It comes from the STE's
   sample sound hardware and differs
   from machine to machine. It does
   not show in emulators.
