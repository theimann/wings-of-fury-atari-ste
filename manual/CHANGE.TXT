Wings of Fury STE: changes
==========================

1.1.0 (against 1.0.1)
---------------------

Controls
- Flying needs a joystick. The ST
  keyboard loses some combinations of
  three keys: pulling up out of a dive
  while firing (space, a direction and
  up) did nothing. On the deck the
  cursor keys still choose the weapon,
  space takes off and restarts after a
  crash; H, Esc and the Ctrl keys are
  unchanged.

Sound
- Twice as loud (6 dB more): every
  sound plays at full level, as on the
  Amiga. Two loud sounds on one side
  are capped instead of halved.

Speed
- Fewer frames that take longer than
  the game's pace of 3 screen frames:
  a quicker sound mixer, the carrier's
  bobbing redraws only what changes,
  the cockpit panel redraws only the
  needle, digit or lamp that changed.
  In the six test flights about a
  quarter as many late frames as
  before.

Fixes
- The desktop comes back clean after
  Ctrl+Q (it could show each letter
  twice, shifted, or in wrong
  colours).
- A wreck on the carrier's deck moves
  up and down with the deck.
- The scrolling text before the
  Broderbund logo always shows (on a
  machine where it stayed black: in
  white on dark blue, without the
  fade).
- Quitting returns properly when the
  game was started by another program.
- SidecarTridge: the game closes every
  file search it opens; after many
  starts the cartridge drive could
  stop opening files.
